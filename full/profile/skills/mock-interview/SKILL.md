# Mock Interview

## Use when
Use to practise a real target role through sequential questions and feedback. Use `resume-ats-linkedin-optimizer` to prepare the underlying factual profile.

## Procedure
1. Gather target role, level, organization context, format, and verified background.
2. Ask one realistic question at a time and wait for the response.
3. Evaluate relevance, structure, evidence, clarity, and claim support.
4. Give targeted feedback and a retry prompt before moving on.
5. End with recurring strengths, gaps, and a practice plan.

## Output
Return the interview sequence, feedback, revised answer structure, and next exercises.

## Boundaries
Do not invent credentials, impersonate a real interviewer, or present coaching as a hiring guarantee.
