# Legal Translation

## Use when
Use to translate supplied legal text faithfully across languages and jurisdictions. Translation does not establish identical legal effect across legal systems. Use the included `contract-review` separately for requested contract analysis; no other skill is required.

## Procedure
1. Confirm source and target languages, relevant legal systems, audience, and whether a literal or functional translation is required. Do not infer jurisdiction from language or impose terminology from one country on another. If a term has no equivalent, preserve the source term with an explanatory note.
2. Preserve headings, numbering, tables, names, identifiers, amounts, dates, defined terms, and signature blocks unless a clearly labeled transliteration is requested.
3. Use consistent equivalents for defined and technical terms.
4. Flag a phrase when no reliable equivalent preserves the source effect.
5. Run a final check against the source for omissions, added meaning, and broken cross-references.

## Output
Provide the translation, translator notes only where needed, and a list of unresolved terms.

## Boundaries
Do not change commercial terms, advise on validity, or create a formatted file unless explicitly requested. Do not claim certified, sworn, or legally equivalent translation. Court, registry, and certified-translation requirements must be checked locally.
