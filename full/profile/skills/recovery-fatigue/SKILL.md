# Recovery and Fatigue

## Use when
Use to decide whether to train, maintain load, reduce volume or intensity, rest, deload, or return gradually after non-diagnostic fatigue or underperformance. Routine design is outside this skill; ask for the existing routine and assess recovery decisions directly without depending on another skill.

## Procedure
1. Check recent training load, performance trend, sleep, soreness, illness signs, stress, and nutrition context.
2. Screen for urgent or persistent symptoms that require clinical care.
3. Distinguish a transient hard session from accumulating fatigue using trends rather than one bad day.
4. Choose the smallest justified adjustment and define when to reassess.

## Output
Return the likely training-state explanation, immediate load decision, recovery priorities, warning signs, and reassessment point.

## Boundaries
Do not diagnose disease, injury, overtraining syndrome, or mental-health conditions.
