# Clear and Concise Writing

Adapted from obra/the-elements-of-style. The bundled `elements-of-style.md` is an English-language reference, not a universal grammar. Apply this workflow when asked to write or revise prose; do not load it for every reply.

## When to use
Use for unclear, redundant, disorganized, or wordy text. Preserve technical, legal, creative, and personal distinctions rather than treating all writing as advertising.

## Prerequisites
No dependency or sibling skill is required. Work with supplied text or approved files through `read_file`, `write_file`, and `patch`.

## Procedure
1. Identify the language, audience, purpose, register, and requested level of editing.
2. Preserve facts, identifiers, quotations, defined terms, uncertainty, and the author's voice. Do not silently strengthen claims or change commitments.
3. Put the main point where readers can find it. Prefer concrete language, useful transitions, coherent paragraphs, and parallel structure where natural.
4. Remove redundancy and empty qualifiers without removing necessary nuance. Use active voice when it clarifies responsibility; keep passive voice when justified.
5. Follow the target language's actual syntax, punctuation, and conventions. English comma rules, articles, word order, and historical usage do not automatically apply to Spanish or other languages. Consult an appropriate language authority if a disputed rule matters.
6. For technical or legal text, distinguish stylistic changes from changes in meaning. Flag ambiguity rather than deciding it without authorization.
7. Review the revision against the original. Explain only consequential changes unless a detailed critique is requested.

## Output
Return the agreed revision or feedback, with substantive changes and unresolved ambiguity identified.

## Pitfalls
Concise does not mean stripped of evidence, warmth, or personality. Do not modernize protected quotations or replace the author's register mechanically. Older English examples are historical illustrations, not endorsements or current usage authorities.

## Verification
Check that meaning, tone, names, numbers, references, and necessary caveats survived; verify improved readability in the target language.
