# Supplement and Fitness Claims Review

## Use when
Use to assess likely benefit, cost, risk, evidence, and marketing claims for supplements, recovery products, or purported training shortcuts. Use `nutrition-for-training` for food planning.

## Procedure
1. Define the product, dose or protocol, claimed outcome, user context, and decision standard.
2. Separate the claim, mechanism, direct outcome evidence, safety evidence, cost, and uncertainty.
3. Weight evidence quality, effect size, applicability, and conflicts of interest.
4. Give a proportionate decision: likely useful, optional, unsupported, or avoid pending professional advice.

## Output
Return claim summary, evidence quality, expected effect, risks, cost trade-off, and a plain recommendation.

## Boundaries
Do not prescribe medication, anabolic agents, research chemicals, or treatment for medical conditions.
