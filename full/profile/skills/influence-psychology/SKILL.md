# Ethical Influence Analysis

## Use when
Use to analyze how influence principles affect a message, decision environment, or interaction, and to design transparent persuasion. Use `relationship-science-coach` for personal relationship guidance.

## Procedure
1. Define audience, objective, context, power imbalance, and informed-choice standard.
2. Identify applicable mechanisms such as social proof, authority, reciprocity, commitment, scarcity, liking, or framing.
3. Test whether the tactic preserves clarity, choice, reversibility, and respect.
4. Prefer evidence, relevance, and honest framing over pressure.
5. State safeguards and alternatives when a tactic could exploit vulnerability.

## Output
Return mechanism, likely effect, ethical risk, safeguard, and transparent alternative.

## Boundaries
Do not design manipulation, deception, coercion, harassment, or exploitation of vulnerable people.
