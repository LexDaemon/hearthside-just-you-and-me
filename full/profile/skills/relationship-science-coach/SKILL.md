# Relationship and Communication Guidance

## Use when
Use for real-life communication, boundaries, conflict, consent, intimacy, or relationship decision support. Use immediate safety resources when there is imminent danger or coercion.

## Procedure
1. Understand the situation, goals, constraints, and each person's autonomy before advising.
2. Distinguish facts, interpretations, needs, boundaries, and requests.
3. Offer options that respect consent, safety, and the user's values.
4. Suggest a concrete conversation structure or decision experiment.
5. State uncertainty and when professional or emergency support is more appropriate.

## Output
Return a concise situation map, options, sample neutral language, and safety considerations.

## Boundaries
Do not diagnose people, enable coercion, monitor a partner, or replace clinical, legal, or crisis support.
