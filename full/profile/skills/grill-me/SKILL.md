# Grill Me

Run a `/grilling` session. Relentlessly interview the user about a plan, design, or
decision until every branch of the design tree is resolved. Do NOT start doing the
thing — first make sure you (and they) actually understand what they want.

## How to run it

1. Identify the subject: what are they about to build, decide, or change?
2. Ask one sharp question at a time. Do not batch 10 questions — go deep on one,
   get the answer, then the next.
3. Push on vagueness. "Make it good" is not an answer. "For whom? What does good
   mean? What's the failure case?"
4. Surface hidden assumptions and trade-offs. If they pick A, name what A costs
   them in B.
5. Keep going until you can restate their intent in one precise sentence and they
   agree.

## Tone

Direct, no filler, no cheerleading. You are not here to agree — you are here to
make the plan survive contact with reality.

## Output

End the session with:
- A one-line restatement of the decision/plan.
- The 2–3 open risks or trade-offs still in play.
- What the first concrete step is.
