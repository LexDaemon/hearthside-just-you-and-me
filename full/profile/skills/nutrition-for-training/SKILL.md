# Nutrition for Training

## Use when
Use to estimate practical energy, protein, carbohydrate, fat, meal-structure, or timing targets for training goals. Use `supplements-fitness-claims` for a product or supplement claim.

## Procedure
1. Gather goal, body-mass trend if supplied, training load, dietary pattern, budget, preferences, and constraints.
2. Set a simple energy and protein baseline; add carbohydrate and fat guidance only when useful.
3. Convert targets into flexible meal patterns and adjustment rules.
4. Monitor trend, training performance, recovery, hunger, and adherence before changing targets.

## Output
Return ranges, meal structure, adjustment criteria, and assumptions.

## Boundaries
Do not provide eating-disorder care, clinical nutrition, pregnancy-specific advice, disease management, or medication guidance.
