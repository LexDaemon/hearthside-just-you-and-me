# Evidence standard for nutrition for training

Nutrition guidance should improve the user’s actual decision: food choice, meal structure, energy availability, performance, recovery, body-mass trend, adherence, or budget. Do not replace medical nutrition care with generic sports-nutrition language.

## Decision standard

1. Use estimates transparently: name inputs, formula or method, units, and uncertainty.
2. Prefer sustainable patterns over precision theatre.
3. Do not infer a clinical condition, body-composition measurement, energy expenditure, or nutrient deficiency from chat.
4. Use `web_search` and `web_extract` to check current consensus statements and systematic reviews for disputed numerical claims or health-consequential advice; cite the source, date, population, and uncertainty. Refer special populations to appropriate professional care rather than requiring another skill.

The dates above matter: retrieve newer reviews when an answer depends on a claim that may have changed.
