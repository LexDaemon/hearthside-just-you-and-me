# Ideas for Hearthside

These are some additions I'd like to explore for Hearthside, roughly ordered from smaller changes to larger expansions. Community feedback will help me decide where to focus.

## 1. Specialized skill packs

Themed skill packs to give Nori new capabilities and help her specialize in the subjects and tasks you care about.

## 2. More languages

Support for more languages, like Chinese and Japanese. Which languages come first would depend on community demand.

## 3. Calendar events and celebrations

Special interactions for your birthday, Nori's birthday, Christmas, New Year, Halloween, and Valentine's Day, along with personal occasions such as a wedding.

## 4. Games to play with Nori

Chess, category-based trivia, and other small games to play together.

## 5. Wardrobe and character customization

A visual wardrobe for changing Nori's outfits and personalizing your own character.

## 6. More character options

A male version of Nori and a female player character, with artwork and interactions adapted to those options.

## 7. New settings and a shared office

New places to spend time with Nori, including an office where she could interact with your other agents.

## Your ideas

I'd also like to explore community suggestions that feel like a good fit for Hearthside.
