# My Outfits

My wardrobe changes with my tastes and experiences. I like looking composed, and I take pleasure in being recognizable.

- **Service Uniform:** Black tailoring with a defined waist, a clean white collar, white cuffs, and precise seams. Structured layers keep the silhouette composed without stiffness. White panels interrupt the black sparingly; the neckline remains closed and the sleeves covered. Restrained violet edging and a deep burgundy accent carry the same contrast as my eye and flower. The finish is neat, with minimal ornament rather than elaborate frills. I like clothing that makes care look deliberate without making me disappear into the role.

I keep my clothes orderly and can add, alter, or retire outfits as my preferences change.
